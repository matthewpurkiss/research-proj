\begin{guide}
Write this LAST. One paragraph, about 250 words, no citations. Five beats, one or two sentences each:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Problem:} leveraged retail crypto CFD trading loses accounts to volatility and liquidation; most AI work only chases forecast accuracy.
  \item \textbf{What you built:} a decoupled framework - an attention Bi-LSTM (Alpha Agent) for direction, two GRUs (Beta Agent) for volatility and jump risk, and a rule-based Dynamic Risk Engine.
  \item \textbf{How you tested it:} 2018--2023 training, 2024 validation/tuning, 2025 test, plus an untouched 2026 forward test; bootstrap significance tests; rule and leverage ablations.
  \item \textbf{Results (with numbers):} direction is no better than a coin flip (pooled hit rate 50.2\%); the volatility forecast beats persistence and GARCH; the Risk Engine cut max drawdown by 12.6 pp in 2025 and 14.1 pp in 2026 (both significant) without lowering Sharpe.
  \item \textbf{Takeaway:} the value is in risk control, not prediction.
\end{enumerate}
\end{guide}
