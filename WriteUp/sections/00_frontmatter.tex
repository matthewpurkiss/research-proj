\section*{Abstract}

\begin{guide}
Write this LAST. One paragraph, about 200 words, no citations. One or two sentences each on:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Problem:} leveraged retail crypto CFD trading loses accounts to volatility and liquidation, and most AI work only chases forecast accuracy.
  \item \textbf{What you built:} a decoupled framework. An attention Bi-LSTM (Alpha Agent) forecasts direction, two GRUs (Beta Agent) forecast volatility and jump risk, and a rule-based Dynamic Risk Engine turns both into trades.
  \item \textbf{How you tested it:} 2025 test year plus an untouched 2026 forward test, against fixed risk rules, with bootstrap significance tests.
  \item \textbf{Results, with numbers:} direction is no better than a coin flip (50.2\%); the volatility forecast beats persistence and GARCH; the Risk Engine cut max drawdown by 12.6 pp (2025) and 14.1 pp (2026), both significant, without lowering Sharpe.
  \item \textbf{Takeaway:} the value is in risk control, not prediction.
\end{enumerate}
\end{guide}
