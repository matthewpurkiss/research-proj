\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Motivation:} crypto CFDs give retail traders leverage on a market that moves 5--10\% in a day, so one bad day can wipe an account. Most deep-learning trading studies report forecast accuracy and stop there, but accuracy does not prevent liquidation.
  \item \textbf{Approach and research question:} separate ``which way'' from ``how risky'', and let the risk forecast drive size, stop and leverage. Ask: does this cut drawdown and liquidations compared with fixed rules, without lowering risk-adjusted returns?

  \item \textbf{Contributions and outline}, in two or three sentences: the framework and CFD backtest; the volatility-target finding; a drawdown result that held on unseen 2026 data; the honest negative result on direction.
\end{enumerate}
