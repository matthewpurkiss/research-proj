\begin{guide}
\textbf{About 1.5--2 pages.} The proposal's background chapter is far too long for this. Condense it to one paragraph per topic, keeping only what the rest of the report relies on, with citations from the proposal's \texttt{.bib}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Recurrent networks for financial forecasting:} LSTM, GRU, Bi-LSTM, and the mixed track record on next-day returns.
  \item \textbf{Attention} in sequence models and in finance.
  \item \textbf{Multivariate and cross-asset features:} macro, on-chain, correlations.
  \item \textbf{Volatility and jumps:} volatility clustering, GARCH(1,1), jump risk. Volatility is forecastable even when returns are not, and a forecast must beat persistence (``tomorrow looks like today'') to count.
  \item \textbf{Risk management for leveraged trading:} volatility-scaled sizing and stops, liquidation, drawdown and Sharpe. Mention the EU 2:1 retail cap on crypto CFDs and the UK's retail ban, citing the regulators.
\end{enumerate}
End with a two-sentence \textbf{gap}: prior work optimises forecast accuracy; few studies evaluate a volatility-driven risk layer end to end under realistic leveraged-CFD conditions.
\end{guide}
