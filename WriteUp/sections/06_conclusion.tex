\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

\begin{guide}
\textbf{About half a page.} Answer the research question in the first sentence, give one sentence per hypothesis, then the takeaway: in this setting, the value of AI lies in forecasting risk, not direction. Close with three or four concrete items of future work:
\begin{itemize}
  \item cap the stop inside the liquidation distance;
  \item recalibrate the volatility forecast on validation data;
  \item model slippage and financing costs;
  \item test on more assets and longer periods.
\end{itemize}
The framework is modular, so a better direction model could be swapped in without touching the risk side.
\end{guide}
